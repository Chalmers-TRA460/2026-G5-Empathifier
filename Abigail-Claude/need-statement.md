# Need Statement

## Final Need Statement

> "A way to improve communication tone in online portal messaging in adult patients communicating with clinicians through government-operated health portals in Sweden in order to improve patient-reported measures of respect and trust in care."

---

## Components

**Problem:** Improve communication tone in online portal messaging
- Clinicians under time pressure write terse portal messages that convey a harsh tone
- Tone (warmth/empathy) is the primary issue — messages are medically accurate but emotionally cold
- Scoped to portal messaging specifically; other communication media (video, in-person) have different mechanisms

**Population:** Adult patients communicating with clinicians through government-operated health portals in Sweden
- Sweden/1177 scoped for cultural, linguistic, and integration reasons
- Problem experienced similarly across adult patients regardless of age or chronic illness status
- Key decision-makers: hospital management, government bodies (e.g., 1177)
- Key influencers: clinicians (doctors, nurses), hospital administrators

**Outcome:** Improve patient-reported measures of respect and trust in care
- Tied to "Respect and treatment" category of Nationell patientenkät (NPE)
- Poor communication tone leads to provider-switching, second-opinion seeking, and relationship erosion

---

## Key Context

- Initiated by a primary care physician; validated across multiple clinicians at one Swedish hospital
- Root cause: growing portal message volume overwhelms clinicians, leaving insufficient time for empathetic responses
- Clinician time pressure is a known constraint but intentionally excluded from the need statement (documented here for stakeholder context)
- Ultimate adoption goal: integration with 1177

---

## Validation Action Items

1. **Interview 2-3 clinicians at different Swedish hospitals** to validate that communication tone — not message volume — is the primary lever for patient trust erosion
2. **Identify a proxy measure for NPE** acceptable on a 6-12 month timeline — consult a Swedish health researcher or 1177 contact
3. **Search for existing portal communication tools** in Swedish or Nordic health systems to understand the competitive landscape and sharpen the unmet need argument
4. **Validate NPE "Respect and treatment" subscale** with 1177 stakeholders as the relevant outcome metric

---

## Original Need Statement (for reference)

> "A way to increase the empathy of clinician responses to patient portal messages for adults in order to improve patient satisfaction."
